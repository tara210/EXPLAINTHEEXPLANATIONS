      How each number is computed

      Attention
      Attention weights of every layer, averaged over heads; the first token (attention sink) is left out and each row rescaled. A word's score is the attention it receives from all later tokens relative to an even spread, then centred per text and per position.
      Value-weighted
      Attention × the size of what the attended token passes on through each head, ‖WO,hvj‖ (Kobayashi et al. 2020), summed over heads.
      Attention × gradient
      Positive part of attention × −gradient of the answer's mean surprisal (Chefer et al. 2021): attention edges that make the explanation easier to predict.
      Occlusion
      Each word removed in turn; how much harder the next 20 tokens become to predict (bits). Measures effect, not looking; expensive, so only on the 5 pairs.
      Word classes
      Causal (because, so, therefore…), contrast (but, however, actually…), illustration (like, imagine, for example…), reformulation (basically, means, in other words…), hedge (kind of, probably, might…), condition (if, when, then…), reader (you, your…), writer (I, my…).
      Caveat
      Attention shows where the model looks, not what drives its output; the measures that include values, gradients or removal get closer to that.
